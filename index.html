Hi, I'm Benjamin Masters

I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Waterloo, working under the supervision of Ewen MacDonald in the Hearing Lab.

My research primarily focuses on understanding and measuring the perception of effort in conversation. I use acoustical, behavioral, and physiological measures (such as pupillometry) to study how cognitive load changes dynamically during interactive speech. Currently, I am involved in multiple projects, including a collaborative initiative with Oticon's Eriksholm Research Centre.
